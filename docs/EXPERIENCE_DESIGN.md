# Experience design: CIRCLE

CIRCLE is an experimental human-state research workbench, **not a game, a therapy, or a medical device**. Its invitation is to understand what the system observed and why it did, held, or refused to do something.

## StoryStack, adapted without gamification

| Layer | Contract |
| --- | --- |
| Fantasy | Become a careful investigator of signals and feedback while retaining personal control. |
| Action | Explore a simulated session; influence permitted scenario or feedback settings; discover the decision trace. |
| Economy | Consent, information quality, independent reproduction, uncertainty, and time spent learning. No health-score rewards. |
| World | A private research workbench, visual/audio mapping, and portable evidence records. |
| Story | An apparent signal change leads to a question: did the controller have sufficient grounds to act? |

## A first investigation

1. **Explore:** show the six-minute recorded simulation and label it as simulation before displaying any interpretation.
2. **Influence:** explicitly enable optional audio or change permitted workbench controls. Consent must remain revocable; a disabled control should say why.
3. **Discover:** inspect a decision, the available evidence, the reason for holding, and the reproduction verdict. A missing result is itself informative.

## Scientific and human boundaries

- Never represent modeled sensor values as measured human signals or modeled outputs as proven therapeutic effects.
- Keep real phone-camera recordings distinct from the simulated workbench and from untested live pathways.
- Preserve quality gating, explicit consent, limits on feedback, and the right to refuse.
- No gamified pressure to change physiological state, disclose data, or prolong a session.
- All sessions should be understandable without sound or decorative motion; prioritize mobile and keyboard paths.

## Acceptance questions

Can a newcomer distinguish simulation from recording, choose an action, see what changed, and trace a refusal to its cause? Can they leave or revoke consent without friction?
